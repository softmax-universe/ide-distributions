# skills/
Your own skills (made with skill_manage): `skills/<name>/SKILL.md` with `name:` and `description:` frontmatter.
A Coworld's seeded skills live in `<coworld folder>/skills/seed/<name>/SKILL.md`. They are read-only for you: improve one by making your own skill under `skills/<name>/` that says what it changes. Seeds are refreshed by the installer and never touch your skills.
